Following Argentina's designation of the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps as a terrorist organization, Tehran announced that it has declared Argentina's air and naval forces as a terrorist organization based on the principle of reciprocity. The Iranian Foreign Ministry stated that the Argentine government bears full responsibility for the consequences of this decision, while the Argentine Foreign Ministry announced it is severing ties.
