Protesters in New York call for arrest of Yoav Gallant

Pro-Palestinian demonstrators called for the arrest of former Israeli Defense Minister Yoav Gallant during his visit to New York.

Alboroto deskThe Jerusalem Post19 November 2026, 15:16
WhatsAppTelegramX

At the protest outside Penn Station, drums were played, banners were unfurled, and the International Criminal Court's arrest warrants against him were held up. Members of the Neturei Karta sect joined the rally, and a group of counter-protesters got into an argument with them, with no arrests made. The protest, led by the Palestinian Youth Movement and the Party for Socialism and Liberation, was also supported on social media by the Jewish Voice for Peace group. Gallant served as defense minister during Hamas's October 7, 2023 attacks and the subsequent war in Gaza, and was dismissed in November.
