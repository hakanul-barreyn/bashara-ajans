US attempt to tighten blockade on Iran met with resistance from neighboring countries

According to a report by The New York Times, the US demanded that neighboring countries close their border crossings to cut off Iran's land trade routes; however, countries such as Iraq, Turkey, and Pakistan resisted this pressure.
