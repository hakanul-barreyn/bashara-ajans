Turkey and Qatar Support Syria's Stability and React to Israel

Alboroto deskSyria TV14 November 2026, 01:17
WhatsAppTelegramX

The stability and reconstruction of Syria were emphasized in a joint communique released after the 12th meeting of the Turkey-Qatar High Strategic Committee held in Istanbul. During the talks chaired by President Recep Tayyip Erdogan and Qatari Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, support for a Syrian-led inclusive political process was reiterated. The communique expressed the importance of the country's recovery through infrastructure, housing, and energy projects, while stating that the international community must take a decisive stance against Israel's violations of Syria's sovereignty. The parties also called for compliance with the 1974 Disengagement of Forces Agreement and the withdrawal of Israeli forces from occupied territories.
