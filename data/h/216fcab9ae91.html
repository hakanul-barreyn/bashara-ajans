Israeli president Herzog and Reza Pahlavi speak at joint seminar

Israeli president Isaac Herzog and Reza Pahlavi met at a webinar organized by the Chaim Herzog Center for Middle Eastern Studies and Diplomacy at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev.

According to a report published by an Israeli embassy, Pahlavi addressed Zionists as "my friends" and said Israel is a source of light for the region. Herzog said his wars are with the regime, not the Iranian people, and thanked Pahlavi for his message of solidarity on the third anniversary of October 7 and for visiting the Auschwitz death camp. Pahlavi claimed the regime must be overthrown and that forty thousand people are fighting the regime, and [[ATLANDI: İkinci, çok daha büyük bir soykırım başlatmaktan çekinmeyecektir.]] The discussion emphasized the historical ties between the two nations and called for a coalition against radicalization.
