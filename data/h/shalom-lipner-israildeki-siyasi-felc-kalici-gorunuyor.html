Shalom Lipner: İsrail'deki siyasi felç kalıcı görünüyor

The National'da yayımlanan analizde yazar Shalom Lipner, 27 Ekim'deki seçimlerin üzerinden üç haftadan fazla süre geçmesine rağmen İsrail'de hükümet kurulamadığını ve siyasi gerginliğin sürdüğünü anlatıyor.

Shalom Lipner: Political paralysis in Israel appears permanent

In an analysis published in The National, author Shalom Lipner explains that more than three weeks after the October 27 elections, a government has not been formed in Israel and political tension persists.

Alboroto deskThe National19 November 2026, 17:00
WhatsAppTelegramX

According to the author, Gadi Eisenkot, who was tasked by President Isaac Herzog with forming a government on November 8, faces only tenuous options and must bring other politicians on board to oust Benjamin Netanyahu, which entails difficult compromises on core positions. The author notes that pulling ultra-Orthodox groups to Eisenkot's side may be unacceptable for figures like Naftali Bennett, who particularly oppose military exemptions. The article concludes that no viable solution to this puzzle is in sight.
