Debate over whether Netanyahu will follow the Theodore Roosevelt example

As coalition negotiations continue in Israel, the question of whether Benjamin Netanyahu will hand over leadership to keep his party in power, like American President Theodore Roosevelt, has come to the agenda.

Alboroto deskThe Jerusalem Post14 November 2026, 21:47
WhatsAppTelegramX

After the 1904 elections, Roosevelt announced he would not run again and supported William Howard Taft, ensuring the Republicans remained in power. Netanyahu had previously stated that he adopted Roosevelt's advice to "speak softly and carry a big stick" against the Iranian threat. As Gadi Eisenkot tries to form a new government, it is wondered whether Netanyahu will step down from the Likud leadership.
