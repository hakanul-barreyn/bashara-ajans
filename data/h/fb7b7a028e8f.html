2030 Dünya Kupası Finali İçin Fas ve İspanya Arasında Rekabet

Competition Between Morocco and Spain for the 2030 World Cup Final

The struggle to host the final match of the 2030 World Cup has taken on a political dimension. Spanish People's Party leader Alberto Nunez Feijoo claimed that if the final is awarded to Morocco, Spain could withdraw from the tournament. Meanwhile, Morocco aims to stand out in this ambitious race with its under-construction giant 115,000-seat Hassan II Stadium project.
