Jerusalem Post columnist questions the loyalty of Shin Bet chief Zini

The author recalls the warnings of those who initially opposed the appointment of Shin Bet head David Zini and states that they initially trusted Zini as well.

Alboroto deskThe Jerusalem Post20 November 2026, 14:49
WhatsAppTelegramX

They write that following the October election, the question of whether Zini is loyal to Prime Minister Netanyahu or the state is now more thought-provoking. According to the author, a police investigation into alleged voter fraud in the Arab community, the involvement of individuals linked to the Prime Minister's Office, and Zini's own stance raise serious questions about the independence of institutions meant to protect democracy in Israel. The article cites examples such as the raiding of homes of Arab activists connected to the Ra'am party on election day and the detention of Dr. Saef Abu Raash, noting that Abu Raash was released unconditionally by the court after being held for six days. The author mentions former president Reuven Rivlin calling Abu Raash to apologize as a positive example. According to the piece, the manner in which the investigation was conducted and its political exploitation must be further examined.
