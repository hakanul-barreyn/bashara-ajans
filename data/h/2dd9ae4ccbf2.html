İsrail'de Hükümet Kurma Görevi Gadi Eisenkot'a Verildi

İsrail Cumhurbaşkanı Isaac Herzog, koalisyon çoğunluğunun kaybedilmesinin ardından hükümet kurma görevini Gadi Eisenkot'a verdi. Yayınlar bu adımı Netanyahu için siyasi bir gerileme olarak nitelendirirken, koalisyon müzakereleri ve olası senaryolar üzerindeki tartışmalara dikkat çekti.

Task of Forming Government in Israel Given to Gadi Eisenkot

Israeli President Isaac Herzog gave the task of forming a government to Gadi Eisenkot following the loss of the coalition majority. Publications described this move as a political setback for Netanyahu while drawing attention to discussions on coalition negotiations and potential scenarios.

Palestinian lens 1relaying 13

Following the elections held in Israel on October 27, President Isaac Herzog announced that he would task opposition leader and former Chief of General Staff Gadi Eisenkot with forming the new government. While efforts among parties continue to secure an absolute majority in parliament, the number of MPs supporting Eisenkot reached 67 with the support of Arab parties.
