Casualties rise in attacks near Sanaa in Yemen

It was reported that the death toll rose and dozens were injured in airstrikes conducted by the Saudi-led coalition forces on the Bani Hushaysh district near the Yemeni capital Sanaa. The Houthis condemned the event, stating that the attacks constitute a war crime.

The Ministry of Health in Sana'a announced that the number of dead and injured in the homes targeted by Saudi attacks in the Beni Hashish district has reached a total of 72, including 21 women and children. Ministry spokesperson Dr. Anis Al-Asbahi reported that search and rescue operations are ongoing.
