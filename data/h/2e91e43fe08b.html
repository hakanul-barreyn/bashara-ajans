Samir Mahmoud, commander of the Qassam Brigades' Rafah Brigade, killed in airstrike

Hamas's military wing, the Izz ad-Din al-Qassam Brigades, announced that Rafah Brigade Commander and General Military Council member Samir Mahmoud (Abu Mahmud) was killed in an Israeli airstrike on Sunday.

Alboroto deskEchorouk15 November 2026, 10:40
WhatsAppTelegramX

In a statement by the Qassam Brigades, it was noted that Mahmoud joined the organization in 1991, previously served time in Israeli prisons, and played a significant role in planning the October 7 attacks. Medics reported that Mahmoud was killed in an airstrike on Al-Mawasi street in the Khan Younis area, and that there were other injuries. The Israeli military stated that it targeted a senior commander of Hamas's military wing in the city of Khan Younis. This assassination marks a continuation of the conflict in the region.
