Raising the Palestinian Flag in Front of the Reichstag in Berlin and Detentions

In Germany's capital Berlin, activists who climbed the flag pole outside the Reichstag building took down the German flag and hoisted the Palestinian flag instead. The activists were brought down and detained by fire and police teams.
