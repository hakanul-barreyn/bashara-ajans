The Washington Post newspaper wrote that US President Donald Trump does not want to restart military attacks on Iran at this stage. Citing a US official, the report stated that negotiations between Iran and the US have made progress in recent weeks. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio argued that the economic sanctions imposed on Iran are very heavy and that there is no need for a military operation. Trump is expected to discuss the Iran issue with Russian and Chinese leaders at the trilateral summit to be held in China next week.
