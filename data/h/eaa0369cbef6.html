Thousands of Migrants Reach El Hierro in Canary Islands

ZBS deskIl Post12 November 2026, 03:00
WhatsAppTelegramX

Thousands of migrants have landed on the small island of El Hierro in Spain's Canary Islands over the past five weeks. The island of 12,000 residents receives heavy migration every autumn because it is a long route from West Africa to Europe where coastal controls can be avoided; in the first half of October alone, 4,357 people reached the island. Stating that 15 of the 30-bed capacity in the island's only hospital are occupied by migrants, hospital director Jéssica Acosta announced that the situation is completely full and alarming. Island government president Alpidio Armas González stated that hundreds of people had to stay in an enclosed space because they could not find shelter, demanding additional emergency housing from the central government.
