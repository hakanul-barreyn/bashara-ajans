The Tunisian Journalists Syndicate published a report within the scope of the day for combating impunity for crimes against journalists. In the report, it was announced that 154 attacks were carried out against industry workers in the last year. Human rights organizations called for the cancellation of decrees restricting freedom of expression and for attacks on journalists not to go unpunished.
