Coast Guard apprehends 46 irregular migrants and rescues 34 people in Izmir

Irregular migrants were caught and rescued in operations organized off the coast of İzmir on November 26, 2026.

In operations carried out by Coast Guard teams in the Seferihisar and Dikili districts, rubber boats were stopped and 46 irregular migrants were caught. In addition, 34 irregular migrants on another boat that was drifting due to a motor failure and requested assistance off the coast of Çeşme were rescued. The migrants brought to the shore underwent health checks and were sent to the Provincial Directorate of Migration Management.
