relaying 13Israeli / US lens 1

The Israeli Prime Minister's Office announced that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu would meet in Jerusalem with Gadi Eisenkot, who has been tasked with forming a new government. The meeting was reportedly held to provide briefings on political and security developments. Meanwhile, it was reported that there is a disagreement between the parties regarding the powers of the transitional government in Israel.
