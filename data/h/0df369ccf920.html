Deutsche Bank documents coincide with right-wing election defeats in Europe and US

Daraj and an international partnership have published an investigation based on a leak of 15 million Deutsche Bank documents.

Alboroto deskDaraj17 November 2026, 09:01
WhatsAppTelegramX

The documents reveal correspondence among financial decision-makers. The investigation is said to be linked to figures including Donald Trump, Jeffrey Epstein and Mansour bin Zayed, and it claims the bank financed development of Israel's Merkava tank for use in Gaza. The project was carried out under the coordination of Barakuda, with the participation of more than 230 journalists from over 50 countries. The text also notes that the publication schedule coincided with the Trump-aligned defeat in the US midterm elections and Netanyahu's defeat in Israel. The investigation covers issues including irregularities in the bank's hiring, suspicious commissions, bribery, and the bank's disregard of warnings about money laundering.
