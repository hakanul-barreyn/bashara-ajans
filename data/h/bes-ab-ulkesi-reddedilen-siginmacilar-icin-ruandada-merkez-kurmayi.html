Five EU countries plan to set up Rwanda center for rejected asylum seekers

Germany, Austria, Denmark, Greece and the Netherlands plan to send irregular migrants whose asylum applications have been rejected and who cannot be returned to their home countries to a "return hub" in Rwanda.

Alboroto deskEFE19 November 2026, 21:01
WhatsAppTelegramX

According to the agreement announced in Vienna, the center is to begin operating in 2027 with a planned capacity of around 100 people. Those staying at the center will not be detained, but they will have to choose between local integration and return to their countries. The agreement has not yet been signed and its details remain to be negotiated. The report says the plan is based on the EU Return Regulation, and that Francesco Pasetti of CIDOB has described the step as the externalization of migration.
