News reports indicate that the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy announced that oil tankers sailing in the prohibited zone east of the Strait of Hormuz with their tracking systems turned off caught fire after hitting sea mines. While Western sources address the incident as "tankers…

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said that Washington's economic pressure on a weakened Tehran is so massive that additional military action is not necessary. The Iranian navy and Revolutionary Guards announced that a fire broke out on three tankers that hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz. US Central Command (CENTCOM) announced that two drones launched towards Bahrain were shot down. The United Nations reported that 25 sailors have been killed in 134 incidents in the strait since February. A young Palestinian was injured as a result of fire opened by Israeli soldiers who raided the village of Jaba near the city of Jenin in the occupied West Bank.
