Syrian Civil Defense teams responded to 11 traffic accidents and 41 fires across the country in a single day. Four people lost their lives in the accidents, while 8 others were injured and transported to hospitals. While there were no casualties in the fires, homes, businesses, and agricultural lands were damaged; the Civil Defense urged drivers to be cautious and take precautions against fire risks.
