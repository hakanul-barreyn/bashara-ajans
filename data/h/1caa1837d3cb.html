Brother of Slain Major Calls on Netanyahu and Opposition for Unity

Menachem Kalmanson, the brother of Major Elhanan Kalmanson who lost his life while saving civilians, wrote an open letter to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, Gadi Eisenkot, and Naftali Bennett.

Alboroto deskArutz Sheva15 November 2026, 11:17
WhatsAppTelegramX

Menachem Kalmanson stated that unity would be possible not just with words, but with painful compromises. He stated that he welcomed the prime minister's call for unity but that concrete steps must be taken. The letter questioned whether they are ready to make sacrifices on issues such as judicial reform and mandatory military service. The author emphasized that a broad-based consensus should be sought for the investigation of the October 7 events.
