Heavy rains effective in Gaza submerged thousands of tents, leaving displaced Palestinians to cope with the first winter storm. According to Al Jazeera, Israeli settlers seized Palestinian homes in Jericho and carried out a destructive raid in the occupied West Bank during this time.
