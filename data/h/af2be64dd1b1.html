Batı Şeria'da Zeytin Hasadına Yönelik Yüzlerce İhlal Belgelendi

According to a report by the Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission, 674 attacks targeting olive trees and farmers were recorded in the West Bank and Jerusalem between October 1 and November 6. According to the released data, a large portion of the attacks concentrated in Nablus, Ramallah, and Salfit regions.
