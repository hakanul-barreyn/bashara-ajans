Marco Rubio Accuses ICC of Illegitimacy and Defends Expansion

Alboroto deskEFE14 November 2026, 02:51
WhatsAppTelegramX

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio argued that the International Criminal Court violates his country's sovereignty, describing the court as fake and illegitimate. Speaking at an event in Washington, Rubio stated that international law has become a tool serving the interests of elites. Emphasizing that the US has possessed the will for expansion and growth since its founding values, the secretary defended the sanctions imposed on the court by the Trump administration in February 2025. The Washington administration does not recognize the jurisdiction of the ICC due to investigations regarding soldiers in Afghanistan and Israeli officials.
