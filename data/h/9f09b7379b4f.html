Tens of Thousands Protested Israel in Amsterdam

Large protest demonstrations were held in Amsterdam, the capital of the Netherlands, with the participation of tens of thousands of people against Israel's attacks and policies in Gaza. Demonstrators demanded stronger action from the Dutch government.

Thousands of activists gathered at Amsterdam's Museumplein square, calling on the Dutch government to take action against the situation in Gaza. The protest, supported by organizations such as Amnesty International and Oxfam Novib, emphasized that approximately two million Palestinians are in urgent need of aid.
