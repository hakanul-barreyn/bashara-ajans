Gadi Eizenkot Tasked with Forming a Government in Israel

Israeli President Isaac Herzog tasked "Yashar" party leader Gadi Eizenkot with forming a new government following the elections. Upon taking over the mandate, Eizenkot called for the establishment of a broad-based national unity emergency government.
