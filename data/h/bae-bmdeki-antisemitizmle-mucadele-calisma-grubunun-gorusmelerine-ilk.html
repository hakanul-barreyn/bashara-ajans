UAE Joins UN Anti-Semitism Working Group Discussions for First Time

The UAE attended discussions of the UN's international working group on combating anti-Semitism for the first time last night, according to Maariv.

Alboroto deskMuhammad al-Sharif25 November 2026, 10:54
WhatsAppTelegramX

The group was established on the initiative of Israeli UN Ambassador Danny Danon. Representatives from dozens of countries took part in the meeting, which identified concrete steps to combat anti-Semitism within UN institutions. These steps will be submitted to the UN Secretary-General, who will take office next year.
