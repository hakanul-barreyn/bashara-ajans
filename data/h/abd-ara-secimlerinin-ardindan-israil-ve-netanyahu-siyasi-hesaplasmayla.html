After US midterms, Israel and Netanyahu face political reckoning

According to an opinion piece in the Jerusalem Post, the period of unquestioned bipartisan support for Israel in the United States is ending.

The article says that after the election, Trump and JD Vance placed responsibility for Republican losses on Israel, and that Trump announced an agreement with Netanyahu to halt military aid by year's end, though the prime minister did not confirm it. The author notes that Abdul El-Sayed, elected in Michigan, advocates restricting aid and technology access to Israel, and that AIPAC spent around $30 million against him. The text says Chris Van Hollen is being promoted to succeed Chuck Schumer, and that Schumer also supports restricting military aid.
