According to the Iranian Revolutionary Guards, two supertankers using an unauthorized route in the Strait of Hormuz and carrying a total of over four million barrels of oil caught fire as a result of blazes that broke out. The incident occurred amid rising tensions and restricted maritime traffic in the region. Authorities continue to examine the details of the incident, which has reignited concerns over the security of energy shipments in the area.
