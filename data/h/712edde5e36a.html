Yemenli Husilerden Suudi Arabistan Tesislerine Yönelik Operasyon İddiası

According to a statement by the Yemen Human Rights Center, a total of 998 casualties have been recorded since July, comprising 379 martyrs and 619 injured. Additionally, it was reported that 52 citizens were martyred and 38 people were injured in attacks on Sanaa and Taiz over the past two days.
