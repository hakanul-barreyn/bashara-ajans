İsrail'in Fransız sol lider Mélenchon'un ülkeye girişini yasaklaması

The Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced on Tuesday that French left-wing politician and 2027 presidential candidate Jean-Luc Mélenchon has been banned from entering the occupied Palestinian territories. The ministry stated that this decision was taken due to Mélenchon's call for French ships to accompany aid fleets attempting to break Israel's blockade of Gaza. The ban comes at a time when tensions in the region remain ongoing.
