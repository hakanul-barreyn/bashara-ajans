Air traffic system fails in the US, affecting thousands of flights

Alboroto deskAlgatedz14 November 2026, 01:46
WhatsAppTelegramX

According to a report by Algatedz, all domestic flights were temporarily suspended due to a widespread failure in the communication systems of the US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). According to Los Angeles Times and FlightAware data, more than 7,800 flights were delayed and approximately 1,000 flights were canceled nationwide. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy stated that the technical team launched an investigation to resolve the issue, while Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullen announced that there was no evidence that the incident was a cyberattack or sabotage.
